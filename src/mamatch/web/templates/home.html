流程 How it works

  1. 卖方访谈与委托核验 Owner interview, mandate and identity check
  2. 上传资料，AI 提取财务数据并提示缺口 Documents uploaded; AI extracts figures and flags gaps
  3. 交易经理与卖方确认匿名简介 Deal manager and seller approve an anonymous teaser
  4. 买方提交收购方向与资金证明并通过审核 Buyers submit a thesis and funding evidence, and are verified
  5. 筛选与 AI 辅助匹配，经审核后引荐 Filtering and AI-assisted matching; introductions after review
  6. 保密协议、卖方同意后开放资料室 NDA, then the data room with the seller's consent
  7. 尽调、意向书、交割与交接，由合作律师、会计师支持 Diligence, LOI, closing and handover with partner professionals
