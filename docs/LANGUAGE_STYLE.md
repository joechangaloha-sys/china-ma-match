# Language editions and mainland finance terminology

Joe's instruction (31 October 2026): English pages use English only; Chinese pages
use Chinese only. Chinese transaction materials should follow professional mainland
investment-banking usage, comparable in register to CICC, CITIC Securities and Guotai.
This is an editorial standard, not an affiliation or endorsement by those firms.

- Keep navigation, headings, notices, controls, document bodies, export text and
  filenames in the selected language. A language selector is also labelled in that
  page's language. Chinese teaser navigation must not open the English prototype.
- Use 项目简介、信息备忘录、保密协议、收购意向书、标的公司、标的股权、
  股权转让对价、企业价值、股权价值、先决条件、尽职调查、排他性磋商、
  法律约束力、交割、过渡安排. Spell out 息税折旧及摊销前利润 in Chinese.
- Use formal, restrained drafting: 拟议、尚待核实、经审核、以正式交易文件为准.
  Distinguish illustrative assumptions from verified facts and approved terms.
  Do not turn disclosures into assurances or alter transaction economics in translation.
- Use 人民币 and 万元, full calendar years and Chinese project names. Identifiers,
  URLs, email addresses and source hashes are data, not translated prose.
- Never rewrite stored party identities or user-supplied deal records to simulate a
  translation. Do not fall back to the other language for product descriptions.
- Public/demo regression checks inspect rendered text, excluding script/style and
  technical hash blocks. Keep live verification separate from template inspection.
